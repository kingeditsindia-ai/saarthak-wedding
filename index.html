01 — Our story

Four chapters, one long yes

  1. June 2019

    A table by the window

    Ananya ducked into a Fort bookshop to wait out a Mumbai downpour and found Saarthak debating a translation of Kabir with the owner. They shared the last dry table, then a cab, then a number each of them saved under the wrong name.

  2. November 2021

    The overnight train

    They took the train to Jaipur with a hotel booked for exactly one night. They still disagree about who got them lost past Hawa Mahal. They agree it was the best wrong turn either of them has taken.

  3. 18 November 2024

    Before the sentence ended

    At Nahargarh Fort, just as the light left the hills, Saarthak asked. Ananya said yes before he finished, which he still pretends to mind. The ring was in his jacket the whole weekend. The jacket was on a chair.

  4. March 2027

    By the water

    They are marrying at Shah Namkeen in Delhi, with both families, a long dinner, and enough music to keep the street awake. You are the guest list they argued over happily.

“We chose one shop in Chander Nagar, a long weekend, and all of you.”

02 — The weekend

Five gatherings, one invitation

Every gathering is at Shah Namkeen in Chander Nagar, Delhi. Times are in India Standard Time. Dress codes are suggestions, not rules — come comfortable and celebrated.

  1. Friday 12 March 3:00 – 6:00 pm

    Mehendi

    Orchid Terrace

    Colour, music, and a long afternoon in the shade. Henna artists arrive at 3. Chai and chaat from 4. Children are very welcome.

    Dress · bright festive, sleeves you can live with once the mehendi dries

  2. Friday 12 March 8:00 pm – midnight

    Sangeet

    Grand Ballroom

    Both families have been rehearsing in secret and denying it. Dinner is seated at 8:30. Dancing is not optional, but it is forgiving.

    Dress · cocktail, jewel tones, something you can dance in

  3. Saturday 13 March 10:00 am – 12:00 pm

    Haldi

    Garden Pavilion

    A morning ceremony in the garden. Yellow is traditional. Stains are part of the photograph, whenever the photographs exist.

    Dress · yellow, ivory, or anything you will not miss

  4. Saturday 13 March 5:30 – 9:00 pm

    Wedding ceremony

    Lakeside Mandap

    The baraat gathers in the forecourt at 5:30. Pheras begin near sunset. Dinner follows on the lawn. This is the hour to be on time for.

    Dress · Indian formal, or black tie if you would rather

  5. Sunday 14 March 7:00 – 11:00 pm

    Reception

    Shah Namkeen

    A seated dinner at Shah Namkeen, a short round of speeches, and a band that has been warned about Saarthak’s uncle.

    Dress · black tie optional

07 — Questions

Before you write to the families

Is this a real wedding?

The wedding venue is Shah Namkeen, C-13, SOM Bazaar Road, Indira Park, near Preet Vihar Metro Station, Chander Nagar, New Delhi 110051. The map on this page marks that shop. The RSVP form stays in this browser only. It does not send a message anywhere.

Are children invited?

Yes at the mehendi and the haldi. The ceremony and reception are easier without the smallest guests. If you need a different plan, say so in the RSVP note.

Can I bring someone?

The invitation is for the people named on it. If a plus-one was intended, the RSVP form lets you count them. When in doubt, call Shah Namkeen on 011 4650 9490.

What should I wear?

Festive colour for Friday afternoon, jewel tones for the sangeet, traditional Indian formal or black tie for the ceremony, and black tie optional on Sunday. The haldi will stain. Plan accordingly and cheerfully.

Where do gifts go?

Your presence is the gift. If you like to send something anyway, the couple would have pointed you to a honeymoon fund. This demo does not take payment.
